Research

My research connects what people say about a system with how that system is designed and tested. I build machine learning approaches, benchmarks and tools that software teams and process analysts can use. I am looking for a postdoctoral group working on process-aware, testable and trustworthy intelligent systems.

Business process management

Linking end-user feedback to process model elements with word embeddings and machine learning. I released six process models, six feedback corpora and gold-standard benchmarks.

Software engineering for IoT

Code quality, testing and data accuracy of wearable apps, including LLM-generated unit tests and real-world use cases with Fitbit devices.

Trustworthy AI agent frameworks

My current direction: agent frameworks whose behaviour is specified, testable and auditable, drawing on process modelling and software testing.
